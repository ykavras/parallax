01Who we are

Parallax is developed by Albert Medya Yazılım ve Dijital Yayıncılık Hizmetleri A.Ş. (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy covers the Parallax app and this website. The Turkish version of this page is also our information notice under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

02What we collect

Account

The email address and account identifier that Sign in with Apple or Google shares with us (with Apple’s Hide My Email we only see a relay address), the username you choose, your language setting and your device’s time zone.

We never see your Apple or Google password, and we don’t store your name.

Conversations

Everything you write to personas, their replies, and when each message was sent.

Game progress

Moves used, hints opened, restarts, results (whether and when you reached the goal, and in how many moves), XP, streaks, your in-game Volts balance, and in-app events such as starting or restarting a conversation.

Notifications

Only if you allow notifications: your device’s push token, your notification settings and your in-app notification inbox.

Technical

Your IP address, which your device reveals whenever it connects to our servers. We use it briefly to protect the service (for example, to rate-limit sign-in attempts) and don’t store it in our database; server logs may keep it for a short time. If error monitoring is enabled: crash and error reports with your device model, operating system, app version, account ID and details of the error.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, health or payment data, or advertising identifiers, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

03How we use it

  • To run your account: signing you in, keeping your progress and applying your language.
  • To run the game: showing today’s persona, generating replies, checking whether you reached the goal, counting moves and opening hints.
  • To show results: your own result, plus anonymous totals such as the number of players, the share who reached the goal and the average number of moves.
  • To keep Parallax safe: preventing abuse and attempts to manipulate the AI, enforcing limits and our Terms of Use.
  • To fix and improve Parallax: finding bugs and tuning personas and game balance. Authorised team members may read conversations when this is needed, for example to investigate a problem you reported.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We don’t use your data for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

04AI and your messages

Personas are fictional characters whose replies are generated by a large language model run by a third-party AI provider. When you send a message, our server sends the provider the persona’s instructions and the recent part of your conversation (up to your last 40 messages and replies). A separate check sends your latest message and the persona’s reply to decide whether you reached the goal.

We do not send your email address, username, account ID or device details to the AI provider. The provider processes the text on our behalf to return a reply and may keep it for a limited period under its own terms, for example to monitor abuse.

AI provider OpenRouter, Inc. (USA)

OpenRouter forwards each request to the underlying model provider we have selected, which also processes the text to generate the reply.

Anything you type is sent to the AI provider. Please don’t share personal information about yourself or others in your conversations.

05Legal bases

We process your data because it is necessary to provide the game you signed up for (performance of a contract); for our legitimate interests in keeping Parallax secure, understanding how it is played and improving it; to comply with legal obligations; and with your consent where it is required, for example for push notifications. These correspond to Article 5(2)(c), (ç) and (f) and Article 5(1) of the KVKK and Article 6(1)(b), (c), (f) and (a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

06Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

Hetzner Online GmbH

Hosts our servers and database in Helsinki, Finland (EU).

OpenRouter, Inc.

Routes your messages to the AI model that generates persona replies (USA). See section 04.

Apple and Google

Sign-in; Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications; when you delete an Apple-linked account we ask Apple to revoke Parallax’s sign-in access.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)

Error monitoring, when enabled. Receives crash and error reports as described in section 02.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, to competent authorities, or to protect the rights and safety of players and others. If Parallax is involved in a merger or acquisition, data may be transferred as part of it, and we will tell you before this policy changes as a result.

Other players only ever see anonymous totals. They never see your conversations, your email address or your results.

07International transfers

Our servers are in the European Union. Some providers, including our AI provider, Apple, Google and Sentry, may process data in other countries, such as the United States. When data leaves Türkiye or the EU, we rely on the transfer mechanisms that the law provides, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and, where required, your explicit consent, in line with Article 9 of the KVKK and Chapter V of the GDPR.

08How long we keep it

  • Account, conversations and progress: while your account exists. “Start over” clears a conversation from your game; we may keep a copy with your account for safety and quality until the account is deleted.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your email address, username, Apple or Google identifiers, push tokens and conversations. We keep de-identified game statistics that can no longer be linked to you (they keep the public totals correct) and records we are legally required to keep.
  • Server logs: rotated automatically and kept only for a short period.
  • Error reports: deleted automatically by Sentry, at the latest after 90 days.
  • Backups: any residual copies are deleted as backups are replaced.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • find out whether we process your data and get a copy of it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly;
  • know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom data is transferred;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and have data deleted;
  • ask us to notify those third parties of any correction or deletion;
  • object to a result that goes against you and arises solely from automated analysis;
  • restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format (GDPR);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye or your local data protection authority in the EU.

To use these rights, email info@albertsoftware.com, preferably from the email address linked to your account. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We reply free of charge within 30 days.

10Deleting your account

  1. Open Parallax and tap PARALLAX at the top of the screen.
  2. In Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the same Apple or Google account you signed in with.

Deletion is immediate and can’t be undone. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. If you can’t open the app, email us and we will delete your account within 30 days. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Parallax in your Apple Account settings.

11Security

Data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Access to our systems is limited to authorised people who need it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

12Children

Parallax is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13Notifications

Parallax asks for permission before sending notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications → Parallax. When you sign out, we deactivate your device’s push token.

14This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts. If you tap EN or TR, your browser remembers that choice locally; it never leaves your device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

15Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

16Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: info@albertsoftware.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact your data protection authority.
