02Frequently asked questions

How does Parallax work?

Each cycle, one person is live for everyone in the world. Read their story, look at the goal, and write whatever you want to say. Every message you send is a move. Reach the goal and the two images align: Cracked.

Is the person I’m talking to real?

No. Every persona is a fictional character. Their replies are generated in real time by AI, shaped by the character’s story, personality and limits. AI can make mistakes or answer unexpectedly, so please don’t share personal information in your conversations.

I’m out of moves. What now?

Moves are limited each day. When you run out, the conversation waits until your moves refill the next day, as long as the person is still live.

When does the next person arrive?

The time left is shown at the top of the persona screen. When the cycle closes, everyone meets the next person at the same moment.

What do hints do?

Hints open as you make moves. They help you understand the person; they never hand you the answer.

What does “Start over” do?

It erases the conversation. The person forgets it and meets you as if for the first time. Moves you have already used that day don’t come back.

How do I change the language?

Tap PARALLAX at the top of the persona screen and choose Türkçe or English under Language. By default, Parallax follows your phone’s language.

I can’t sign in.

Parallax supports Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. Check your connection and try again. Apple and Google lead to the same account only when both share the same email address; if you used Apple’s Hide My Email, sign in with Apple again. If it still fails, write to us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications → Parallax.

Something in a conversation upset me or felt inappropriate.

Personas are fictional and their replies are generated by AI, which can occasionally go wrong. Tell us your username, the persona number and roughly when it happened. We’ll look into it and adjust the character if needed.

Parallax is a game, not a support service. If you are going through something difficult yourself, please talk to someone you trust or contact your local emergency services.

03Delete your account

You can delete your account inside the app at any time:

  1. Open Parallax and tap PARALLAX at the top of the screen.
  2. In Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the same Apple or Google account you signed in with.

Your account is deleted together with your conversations, moves, Volts and opened hints. This can’t be undone. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

Can’t open the app? Email info@albertsoftware.com with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days.
